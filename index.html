THE OPERATING SYSTEM

Signals that make every solve count.

CyberYardHub keeps the mechanics visible so practice stays fair, personal, and easy to repeat.

↗01

Dynamic + static scoring

Static challenges keep their published points. Dynamic challenges decay as more operators solve them, with the floor keeping a late solve worthwhile.

◆02

First blood + blood points

The first valid solve earns a first-blood bonus, with blood points rewarding the insight that finds a path early.

◎03

Team play with invite codes

Create or join a team with a rotating invite code, then compare the crew on the team leaderboard using the best result per challenge.

◇04

Prerequisite-locked paths

Let a challenge require a prior solve so missions can build on one another instead of becoming a loose list of targets.

⌁05

Per-user dynamic flags

When enabled, the server derives a distinct flag for each account and challenge, so a shared answer does not transfer.

⟳06

Isolated challenge instances

Challenge instances are registered for operational visibility and can be reset when a run needs a clean, repeatable state.

01

Eligibility

Use CyberYardHub only in an authorized training, education, or research context. Participants must be at least 16 years old or have the documented permission of a parent, guardian, educator, or organization. One account belongs to one person; do not share credentials, sessions, or invite codes outside your team.

02

Solo and team play

Choose solo play for individual practice or create or join a team for shared problem solving. A team may have up to six members. Membership is one-at-a-time, and only the captain sees the rotating invite code. Captains are responsible for keeping membership accurate; captaincy must be transferred before a captain leaves a team with other members.

03

Scoring and first blood

Static challenges award their published point value. Dynamic challenges decay smoothly as more operators solve them, with a published floor so a late solve remains worthwhile. The first valid solve receives a first-blood bonus, recorded once by the server. Team scoring uses the best score for each challenge and the team’s cumulative adjustments; duplicate flags never award points twice.

04

Duplicate submissions

Submit only flags you derived from the challenge you selected. A solved challenge cannot be solved again, and repeat submissions do not add points. If the same challenge is solved by multiple members of a team, only the strongest awarded value contributes to the team total. Rate limits protect the range; repeated automated attempts may result in a temporary lock.

05

Hints and dynamic flags

Hints are optional and may cost points. Unlock only the hint you need and keep the cost in mind. Some challenges use a per-operator dynamic flag: the server derives your flag for that challenge, so a flag shared by someone else is not valid. Never publish a live flag, including in community posts or chat.

06

Conduct and no spoilers

Be curious, accurate, and considerate. Do not attack unrelated systems, access another participant’s data, evade rate limits, or use the community to solicit answers. Do not post flags, complete solution paths, or challenge-specific spoilers before the range owner closes the relevant challenge. Harassment, abuse, and malicious content are not permitted.

07

Reporting a concern

Use the in-product report controls for community content that is spam, abusive, unsafe, or a spoiler. For a challenge, scoring, or platform issue, contact the local range owner with the challenge name, timestamp, and reproducible steps. Reports are reviewed as reports—not as automatic guilt—and good-faith debugging is welcome.

08

Availability and recovery

Service availability may change during an incident. The interface reports temporary changes clearly rather than silently losing work. Completed solves and historical standings remain available for review. The range owner will publish the reason and restoration path through the local notice channel.
